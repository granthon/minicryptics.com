A small one with a fair few different clue types in it — initial letters, a hidden word, a homophone and a cryptic definition all make an appearance.

**Across**

- **1** ||Double definition: "press" (newspapers, informally) and "bits of cloth" are both RAGS.||
- **5** ||The "tips" (first letters) of "Margaret Atwood Kimono Expert" spell MAKE, i.e. "fashion".||
- **6** ||"Taste of grapes" = G + an anagram ("withered") of VINE — GIVEN, i.e. "offered".||
- **7** ||"Down" (sad) = BLUE, which sounds like ("audibly") BLEW, "fluttered".||
- **8** ||Hidden word: "ramshacKLE Edifices" — Paul KLEE, painter.||

**Down**

- **1** ||"Head-covering" = RUG (slang for a wig) + "handy" (near) = BY — RUGBY.||
- **2** ||Cryptic definition: an auctioneer's GAVEL comes down to conclude the sale of a lot.||
- **3** ||"Casserole" = STEW; take out the T (as in T-bone) and put in K for "king" — SKEW, "lean".||
- **4** ||Cryptic definition: the GENIE grants wishes.||
- **5** ||"Factory" = MILL; its final letter L gives way to the letter before it in the alphabet, K — MILK.||
